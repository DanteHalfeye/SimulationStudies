## Design intention

The experience explores how Steam's identity as a digital storefront can be communicated through gameplay rather than through a conventional advertisement or informational interface. Instead of presenting the company through paragraphs, logos, or promotional panels, the design turns its name into the primary play space.

The central idea is that the storefront itself becomes a game about discovering discounts. Players interact with the word STEAM, collect game offers, and accumulate savings. The experience connects the company's identity with its core activity: making games accessible to players through a digital marketplace.
